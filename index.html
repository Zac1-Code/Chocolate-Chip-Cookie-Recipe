Chocolate Chip Cookies

Chocolate chip cookies are a sweet dessert with a soft center and chocolate pieces. Follow this recipe to make your own batch.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter 1 cup butter
  • 1 cup brown sugar 1 cup brown sugar
  • 2 cups flour 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup chocolate chips 1 cup chocolate chips
  • 1 egg 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Let the butter soften before mixing.
  2. Mix the butter and brown sugar in a bowl until smooth. Add the egg and vanilla extract.
  3. Slowly add the flour and stir until a dough forms. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  4. Place spoonfuls of dough on a baking sheet, leaving space between each cookie.
  5. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes, until the edges are lightly golden.
  6. Let the cookies cool before serving. Enjoy your homemade chocolate chip cookies!
